# A group result

All three independent FAST-LIO2 trajectories are complete and monotonic, cover
278.36 s, contain finite poses, and have no gap larger than 0.5 s. No run is
excluded based on its endpoint or ATE.

ATE RMSE values are 7.820619, 13.463294 and 4.363484 m. The mean is
8.549132 m, the sample standard deviation is 4.593439 m, and the median is
7.820619 m. Independent FAST-LIO2 therefore also exhibits substantial
repeatability variance on this sequence.

run02 and run03 lack live parameter dumps; this limitation is documented rather
than reconstructed. The configuration and repository revision are frozen, and
the retained CSVs establish their trajectory coverage.
