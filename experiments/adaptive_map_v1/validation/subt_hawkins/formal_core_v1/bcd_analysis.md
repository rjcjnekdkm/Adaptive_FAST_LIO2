# Formal B/C/D core ablation

Under one frozen frontend revision and replay protocol, C is the best of the
three internal groups. Relative to B, quality/range filtering reduces mean ATE
by 61.34% and sample standard deviation by 43.86%. Adding the current
normal-direction bin policy in D does not improve C: mean ATE rises by 163.14%
and the high-error tail returns.

The defensible conclusion is that the basic quality/range map-insertion policy
has positive evidence on SubT Hawkins, while the incremental benefit of the
current direction-bin policy is not demonstrated. The direction rule rejects
very few points at the current threshold, so these results do not support a
strong mechanism claim about geometric diversity.

These are descriptive three-run results. External FAST-LIO2 A and full-window E
remain separate required comparisons under the experiment plan.
