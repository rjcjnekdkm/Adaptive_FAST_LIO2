# Offroad2 A baseline

All three independent FAST-LIO2 trajectories cover 421.10 s with 4212 finite,
monotonic poses and no gap above 0.5 s. Endpoint differences are centimeter
scale.

The local official GEODE GNSS flow gives ATE RMSE 0.103886, 0.103199 and
0.103125 m. The mean is 0.103403 m and sample standard deviation is 0.000420 m.
Only run01 has live FAST-LIO2 and recorder parameter dumps.
