# Offroad2 B baseline

All three runs cover 421.10 s with 4212 finite poses and no gap above 0.5 s.
Adaptive Map and every adaptive rejection path remain inactive. run01/run02
share the same trajectory; run03 has only a small endpoint difference.

The local official GEODE GNSS conversion and RMSE flow gives ATE RMSE
0.110601, 0.110601 and 0.110699 m. The mean is 0.110634 m and sample standard
deviation is 0.000057 m. B is a stable internal baseline for this sequence.
